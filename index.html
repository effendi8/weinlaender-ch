CAS in AI Management · CAS Thesis · 2026

Reshoring BPO: Replacing Business Process Outsourcing with Generative AI

Three decades of offshoring administrative processes assumed that cheap labor arbitrage was permanent. It wasn't. Agentic AI has changed the equation, and the research points the same way.

Hidden coordination costs, language barriers, and internal workarounds regularly cause fully loaded BPO costs to exceed stated rates by 20 to 30%.

The Research

Five expert interviews across three industrial archetypes (Greenfield/Scaling, Mature/Complex, and Midsize-Enterprise) to identify a universally applicable automation target in industrial supply chain operations.

The Golden Overlap

One process was named by all five companies as a primary pain point: Order Confirmation Management. The only workflow with unanimous confirmation across every archetype, and the ideal beachhead for agentic AI deployment.

The Solution

An agentic AI system built on a Perceive-Reason-Act architecture, hosted on Microsoft Azure, delivered as a SaaS product. It eliminates the offshore middleman and returns control, speed, and data integrity to your own operations.
