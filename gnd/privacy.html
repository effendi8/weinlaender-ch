Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Stefan Weinlaender, 8700 Küsnacht (Zurich), Switzerland, email kontakt@weinlaender.ch.

Swiss law applies, specifically the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), in force since 1 September 2023. Where readers in the EU, EEA or the UK are concerned, we follow the corresponding provisions of the GDPR and UK GDPR.

2. What Good News Daily is

A daily selection of news stories. Each story links to the publisher's original article. There is no registration, no newsletter and nothing for sale.

3. Data processed when you visit this page

Server log data. When you open the page, the hosting provider records your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the page and to detect abuse.

Purpose: secure and reliable operation. Legal basis under GDPR: Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest. Retention is determined by the hosting provider. Hosting provider: Replit, Inc., United States.

Cookies. This page sets no cookies.

Analytics. No web analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising technology, no user profiling.

Fonts. All typefaces are served from this website's own server. No font provider receives your data.

Images. Brand images on this page are loaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), operated by GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company, in the United States. Your IP address is transmitted to GitHub when your browser requests those images. No other data is sent, and GitHub receives nothing else about your visit.

Contact. There is no contact form. If you write by email, your message and address are processed to answer you, and kept as long as the exchange requires.

4. Data processed on our LinkedIn page

LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company, Wilton Place, Dublin 2, Ireland, is responsible for readers in the EEA and Switzerland and processes your data when you visit or interact with our page, under its own privacy policy.

We receive only aggregate statistics from LinkedIn, meaning numbers of views, reactions, comments and shares, never the identity of individual readers. For those statistics LinkedIn and we are joint controllers under the terms LinkedIn publishes in its Page Insights Joint Controller Addendum. Primary responsibility for the platform lies with LinkedIn.

5. People named in stories, and people we quote

Our posts summarise published news articles and can therefore name people who appear in those articles, exactly as the publisher named them. We take these names from the public article, we link to it, we add nothing to what it says, and we hold no other information about the person.

Purpose: reporting on published news. Legal basis: legitimate interest in publishing a factual daily news selection, FADP Art. 31 and GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). Retention: candidate stories including names for at most 30 days in our working records; published stories, with title and link, for as long as the page exists, so the same story is never presented twice.

A person named in a published story may ask us to remove the name or the post. We consider such requests promptly and, where the story concerns a private individual, we remove the name on request.

Posts may close with a short quotation attributed to a public figure, living or historical, with the source held on file.

6. How posts are produced, and who is involved

Stories are gathered from public news feeds and checked against the publisher's article. Summary text is drafted with the help of an AI model and reviewed and released by a person before publication.

Service providers involved in producing and publishing posts, and the data that reaches them:

Anthropic PBC, San Francisco, United States. Receives the text of candidate news articles for verification and summary drafting. Anthropic does not use this content to train its models under its Commercial Terms, and processes it under a data processing addendum incorporating the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with a Swiss addendum.

Buffer, Inc., United States. Schedules and publishes posts to LinkedIn and returns aggregate reader statistics.

Google Ireland Limited, for Swiss and EEA users. Runs the automation, stores working records in Apps Script and Sheets, and sends archive copies by email to the controller only.

Telegram FZ-LLC. Delivers the daily post to the controller for review before publication. No reader data is sent.

Wikimedia Foundation, United States. Wikiquote is consulted, read-only, as a source of attributed quotations.

No reader data reaches any of these providers, apart from the aggregate LinkedIn statistics described in section 4.

7. Transfers abroad

Some providers are in the United States. Switzerland recognises the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies. Where a provider is not certified, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the Swiss addendum. You may ask us for details of the safeguards in place.

8. Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and, where applicable, receive your data in a portable form.

Write to kontakt@weinlaender.ch. We answer within 30 days.

If you are dissatisfied, you may complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, Switzerland, or, if you are in the EU or EEA, to your national supervisory authority.

9. Children

This page is not directed at children, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone under 16.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the way the page works changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
