Why this exists

Progress is continuous. It arrives in small increments, in places that rarely lead the bulletin. A trial reads out. A grid holds power it could not hold last year. A factory runs on something cleaner than it ran on before. These things are real, they compound, and most of them go unreported.

Good News Daily is a daily record of them.

Hard news appears here too, and gets covered straight. The question asked of it is where it opens something, and for whom.

How it is made

Sources. Headlines come from established wire services and papers of record. Reuters, AP, the Financial Times, the BBC, the Wall Street Journal. Where a claim originates with a company, it says so.

Structure. Six items, never more. Three headlines with a short opportunity line each. Two or three positive developments, at least one on climate or technology. Every item links to its source, on the keyword.

Cadence. Working mornings, Europe/Zurich. The first working day of the week opens with a short quote. The last one closes with one.

What it never carries. No affiliate links. No sponsored placements. No paid inclusions. Nothing here earns a commission, now or later.
